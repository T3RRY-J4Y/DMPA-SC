Health information is not medical advice

The site gives general information about DMPA-SC self-injectable contraception. It supports, but does not replace, advice from your nurse or health worker. Your first injection must be given by a trained provider, who will also show you how to self-inject.

Always follow the instructions that come with your device and the advice of your health worker. If you are unsure about anything, or have side effects that worry you, contact your clinic. In an emergency, go to your nearest clinic or hospital.

The 3D demonstrations and illustrations show the steps in a simplified way. They are not exact pictures of any one product.

The date calculator

The next-injection date calculator is a guide only. It works out dates from the information you enter, so please check the result with your health worker or the date on your clinic card. We are not responsible for injections missed or given late because of a date the calculator showed.
