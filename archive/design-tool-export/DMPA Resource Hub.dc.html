What is DMPA-SC?

A new injectable contraceptive that is safe, effective and easy to use. A healthcare provider gives your first injection and shows you how to do the next one, then you can take a unit home to self-inject after 3 months.

Not comfortable injecting yourself? A health provider can do it for you.

  • 99%effective at preventing pregnancy
  • 3 monthsof protection from one injection
  • Just under the skinInjected subcutaneously, not into the muscle, with a small, short needle, often less painful.
  • Fewer clinic visitsOnly every 6 months for refills if you self-inject.
  • Control & privacyFits into your schedule and lifestyle, at home.
  • Trusted worldwideAlready used safely by women in many countries.

What to expect after your injection

Everyone's body is different, and it's normal to notice some changes. Most side effects are mild and go away over time.

Your period may change

Lighter or heavier bleeding, spotting, or your period may stop. This is safe and common. It does not mean you are pregnant or unwell.

Small body changes

Some women notice mild headaches or small changes in weight. These are usually temporary.

A sore injection spot

Slight pain, redness or swelling where you injected should disappear in a few days.

Mood or energy shifts

If changes in mood or desire bother you, talk to your nurse or health worker. Small adjustments can help.

Remember: these effects are normal while your body adjusts. If you have heavy bleeding or anything feels worrying, contact your clinic. You're never alone in this.

Remember your next injection

  • Inject every 12 weeks (about 3 months) to keep preventing pregnancy.
  • Write the date on your reminder card or phone calendar before leaving the clinic.
  • Set reminders a few days before and on the day itself.
  • Keep your next unit somewhere safe and easy to remember, like your toiletry bag or with your sanitary pads.
  • Missed your window? Contact your clinic as soon as possible.
